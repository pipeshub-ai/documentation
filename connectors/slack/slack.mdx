Slack brings your Slack conversations into PipesHub: channel messages, thread replies, and the files and links people share in them. Once synced, you can search them and use them in agents.

There are two Slack connectors. Most organizations start with **Slack Workspace**.

| | **Slack Workspace** | **Slack** (personal) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who sets it up** | An administrator, once for the whole organization | Each person, for their own Slack account |
| **What it brings in** | Public channels, and private channels the Slack app is invited to | Your public and private channels, your direct messages and your group messages |
| **Who can search it** | People who can see the channel in Slack (details below) | Only you |
| **Setup** | [Slack Workspace setup](#step-1-create-a-slack-app-and-get-a-bot-token) | [Personal Slack setup](#personal-slack-connector) |

<Info>
  **Who can search Slack Workspace data:** access follows the Slack channel. Everyone in your Slack workspace (except guests) can search public channels. Private channels are searchable only by their members. The person who set up the connector can search every channel it syncs. See [Who can see what](#who-can-see-what) for the details.
</Info>

## Before you start

- A Slack account that is allowed to create and install apps in your workspace. Some workspaces need an owner or admin to approve new apps.
- Admin access to PipesHub, so you can open **Workspace → Connectors**.
- People's Slack profiles should use the same email address as their PipesHub accounts. PipesHub uses the email address to match people, so it knows who may see which channel.

## Step 1: Create a Slack app and get a bot token

PipesHub reads Slack through a small Slack app that you create. The app gets a **bot token**, a password-like key that starts with `xoxb-`, which you paste into PipesHub.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    Go to the [Slack apps page](https://api.slack.com/apps), sign in, and click **Create New App**. Choose to start from scratch (**Blank app**).

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/salck-1-your-app.png" alt="Slack Your Apps page with the Create New App button" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/slack-2-create-app.png" alt="Create new app dialog with Blank app selected" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the app and choose your workspace">
    Enter an **App name** (for example `pipeshub`), pick the **Workspace** you want to bring into PipesHub, and click **Create**. You can't change the workspace later.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/slack-3-create-app-2.png" alt="Dialog with the app name and the workspace picker" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the bot token scopes">
    Scopes are the permissions you give the app. In the left sidebar, open **OAuth & Permissions**. Under **Scopes → Bot Token Scopes**, click **Add an OAuth Scope** and add each scope below.

    | Scope | Needed? | What it lets PipesHub do |
    |---|---|---|
    | `channels:read` | Required | See the list of public channels |
    | `channels:history` | Required | Read messages in public channels |
    | `groups:read` | Required | See the private channels the app is invited to |
    | `groups:history` | Required | Read messages in those private channels |
    | `users:read` | Required | See who is in the workspace and show names in place of `@mentions` |
    | `users:read.email` | Required | Match Slack people to PipesHub accounts by email, so the right people get access |
    | `files:read` | Required | Read the files shared in messages |
    | `channels:join` | Optional | Join public channels by itself. Without it, the app reads only the public channels you invite it to |
    | `usergroups:read` | Optional | Bring in Slack user groups (such as `@design-team`) and their members. Without it, user groups are not synced |

    You don't need any `im:` or `mpim:` scopes. This connector never reads direct messages or group messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install the app">
    On **OAuth & Permissions**, under **OAuth Tokens**, click **Install to** your workspace. Check the permissions and click **Allow**. If your workspace needs approval for new apps, the request goes to a workspace owner first.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/slack-5-install-to-org.png" alt="OAuth & Permissions page with the Install button under OAuth Tokens" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/slack-6-allow-to-install.png" alt="Slack page asking you to allow the app to access the workspace" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the bot token">
    Back on **OAuth & Permissions**, under **OAuth Tokens**, copy the **Bot User OAuth Token**. It starts with `xoxb-`.

    <Warning>
      Keep this token secret, like a password. Anyone who has it can read what the app can read. If it leaks, click **Regenerate** in Slack and paste the new token into PipesHub.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite the app to private channels">
    Slack never lets an app into a private channel on its own. In each private channel you want in PipesHub, send this message (use your app's name):

    ```
    /invite @pipeshub
    ```

    Public channels: with `channels:join`, the app joins them by itself wherever Slack allows it. Slack can still refuse some joins, for example when a workspace setting blocks it. Any channel the app can't read is named in a **Some Slack channels were not synced** notice after the sync. Invite the app to those channels by hand. Without `channels:join`, invite the app to every public channel you want synced.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you add or change scopes later, Slack only applies them after you reinstall the app (**OAuth & Permissions → Reinstall**). Then paste the token into PipesHub again.
</Note>

## Step 2: Connect Slack Workspace in PipesHub

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the connector">
    In PipesHub, open **Workspace → Connectors**, search for **Slack**, and click **Set up** on the **Slack Workspace** card.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/pipeshub-find-connector.png" alt="Connectors page with Slack Workspace found by search and its Set up button" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the bot token">
    On the **Authenticate** tab, enter an **Instance name** (for example `Slack Workspace`), paste the bot token into **Bot Access Token**, and click **Next**.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/pipeshub-authenticate.png" alt="Authenticate tab with the Instance name and Bot Access Token fields" style={{ display: 'block', margin: '0 auto' }} width="420" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sync and save">
    On the **Configure records** tab, pick how often to sync and which channels to bring in (see [Choose what to sync](#choose-what-to-sync)). Then click **Save configuration**.

    When you save, PipesHub checks the token with Slack. If the token is wrong, refused by Slack, or missing a required scope, the save fails and the message tells you why.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/pipeshub-configure-records.png" alt="Configure records tab with sync settings, Enable Manual Indexing, sync filters and indexing filters" style={{ display: 'block', margin: '0 auto' }} width="420" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the first sync">
    When PipesHub says the instance is ready, click **Start syncing now**. You can also click **I'll do it later** and start it from the connector's page.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-team/pipeshub-ready-to-sync.png" alt="Instance is configured and ready to sync dialog with the Start syncing now button" style={{ display: 'block', margin: '0 auto' }} width="420" />
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what to sync

These settings are on the **Configure records** tab. Click **Add filter** to add the **Channel** and **Channel Types** filters.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync strategy** | **Scheduled** syncs on its own at the interval you pick. **Manual** syncs only when you start it. |
| **Sync interval** | How often a scheduled sync runs. The default is 1 hour. |
| **Sync Messages From** | How far back the first sync goes. The default is the last 30 days; pick an earlier date to bring in older history. Later syncs only fetch new messages. A long history makes the first sync slow, so start small if you can. |
| **Channel** | Bring in only the channels you choose (**is any of**), or every channel except the ones you choose (**is none of**). |
| **Channel Types** | Public channels, private channels, or both. |
| **Enable Manual Indexing** | On: synced messages are not searchable until you choose to index them. Off: everything synced is indexed. |
| **Index Messages**, **Index Threads**, **Index Files**, **Index Links** | Turn one off to keep that kind of content out of search. It is still synced, and you can index items later from **All Records**. |
| **Index Bot Messages** | Turn off to skip messages posted by bots and apps, and threads that a bot started. These are not brought in at all. Bot replies in a thread that a person started are kept. |

## What gets synced and how it stays up to date

**Synced:** messages in the channels the app can read, with reactions and with `@mentions` shown as names; thread replies; files and links shared in messages; workspace members who have an email address on their Slack profile; and Slack user groups (only with the `usergroups:read` scope). Messages sent close together are grouped, so a quick back-and-forth is found as one conversation.

**Not synced:** direct messages, group messages, canvases, lists, pins, bookmarks and huddle transcripts.

**Keeping up to date:**

- Each sync fetches the messages posted since the last sync, channel by channel.
- A scheduled sync also picks up new replies to threads started in about the last 24 hours. Replies to older threads come in on a full sync.
- Edits, deletions and new reactions on messages that were already synced come in only on a **full sync**, for messages inside **Sync Messages From**. A full sync also removes deleted messages and channels.
- When a channel is archived, PipesHub stops reading it. What was already synced stays until the next full sync, which removes it like a deleted channel.
- If Slack is busy or a channel can't be read completely, PipesHub keeps its place and tries again on the next sync. Large workspaces sync gradually because Slack limits how fast apps can read.

### Who can see what

Each message, thread and file gets the access of the channel it was posted in.

- **Public channel:** everyone in the Slack workspace except guests, plus guests who are members of that channel. Guests never see other public channels.
- **Private channel:** only its Slack members.
- **The person who set up the connector** can see every channel the connector syncs, including private channels. This comes from setting up the connector, not from their Slack account.
- **Everyone else gets no access from Slack.** This includes people with no email address on their Slack profile, deactivated Slack accounts, and people from other organizations in shared (Slack Connect) channels. The rule above for the person who set up the connector still applies to them.

People are matched by email. Someone whose PipesHub email differs from their Slack email doesn't get access to their channels.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A private channel is missing">
    Slack doesn't let apps into private channels by themselves. Run `/invite @your-app-name` in the channel, then run the sync again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notice: Some Slack channels were not synced">
    The app can't read the channels named in the notice (it shows the first five, then "and N more"). Invite the app to each of them with `/invite @your-app-name`. For public channels, you can instead add the `channels:join` scope and reinstall the app. If a channel is still listed after that, Slack refused the join, so invite the app by hand.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Saving fails: The Slack token is missing these scopes">
    Add the scopes named in the message under **Bot Token Scopes** (see the [scope table](#step-1-create-a-slack-app-and-get-a-bot-token)), reinstall the app, and paste the token again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notice: Slack token refused">
    Slack no longer accepts the token. It was regenerated or revoked, the app was removed, or the workspace is inactive. The sync stops, and data already in PipesHub stays as it was. Reinstall the app if needed, then paste its current **Bot User OAuth Token** into the connector's settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Invalid Slack token format">
    Paste the **Bot User OAuth Token** from **OAuth & Permissions**. It starts with `xoxb-` (or `xoxe.xoxb-` if the app uses token rotation). A token that starts with only `xoxe-` is a refresh token and won't work here.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Syncing stops about 12 hours after setup">
    Your Slack app has **token rotation** turned on, so its tokens expire after about 12 hours. PipesHub can't renew a pasted token. Create a new Slack app with token rotation left off (Slack doesn't let you turn it off again), and paste its token.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notice: Slack channels could not be listed">
    Slack didn't return the full channel list. The channels it did return were synced, and the next sync tries again.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Someone can't find messages they should see">
    - Check that their PipesHub email matches the email on their Slack profile.
    - Guests only see the channels they are in.
    - Check whether **Enable Manual Indexing** is on, or an **Index** setting is off. Index the records from **All Records** if needed.
    - The first sync may still be running. Check the connector's sync status.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Edits, deletions or new thread replies don't show up">
    A scheduled sync only fetches new messages and replies to recent threads. Run a full sync to pick up edits, deletions and replies to older threads.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nothing older than a certain date">
    The first sync only goes back as far as **Sync Messages From**. Pick an earlier date and run a full sync.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Personal Slack connector

The **Slack** connector brings in what you can see in Slack: your public and private channels, your direct messages and your group messages.

<Info>
  Only you can search what this connector syncs. Sign in with the Slack account that uses the same email as your PipesHub account.
</Info>

You need a Slack app with your own **Client ID** and **Client Secret**. If an administrator already saved a Slack app in PipesHub, you can pick it when you set up the connector and skip steps 2 to 4.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the setup in PipesHub">
    Open **Personal → Your connectors** (or click **Your connectors** on the Connectors page), find **Slack**, and click **Set up**. On the **Authenticate** tab, set the authentication method to **OAuth 2.0** and copy the **Redirect/Callback URL** shown there. It looks like `https://<your-pipeshub-address>/connectors/oauth/callback/Slack`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Slack app and add the redirect URL">
    Create an app as in [Step 1](#step-1-create-a-slack-app-and-get-a-bot-token) (for example `slack-personal-app`). Open **OAuth & Permissions**, click **Add New Redirect URL** under **Redirect URLs**, paste the URL, then click **Add** and **Save URLs**. It must match the PipesHub value exactly.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-individual/slack-4-add-redirect-url.png" alt="Redirect URLs list in Slack with the PipesHub callback URL added" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />

    <Tip>
      Using the [Slack toolset](/toolsets/slack/slack) too? Also add its URL, which ends in `/toolsets/oauth/callback/Slack`, so both can use the same app.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the user token scopes and install">
    On the same page, under **Scopes → User Token Scopes** (not Bot Token Scopes), add all of these:

    ```
    channels:read  channels:history  groups:read  groups:history
    im:read  im:history  mpim:read  mpim:history
    users:read  users:read.email  users.profile:read  files:read
    ```

    Then click **Install to** your workspace and **Allow**.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-individual/slack-5-add-user-scope.png" alt="User Token Scopes list in Slack" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Client ID and Client Secret">
    Open **Settings → Basic Information**. Under **App Credentials**, copy the **Client ID** and the **Client Secret** (click **Show**). Paste them into PipesHub, give the app a name, and continue.

    <img src="/images/connectors/slack-individual/slack-6-app-cred.png" alt="Basic Information page in Slack with the App Credentials section" className="block mx-auto w-11/12" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Slack">
    Sign in to Slack from PipesHub and click **Allow**. Use the Slack account with the same email as your PipesHub account. If PipesHub can't find your email, the sync stops.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to sync and start">
    On **Configure records**, choose your settings as in [Choose what to sync](#choose-what-to-sync). The personal connector also lets you pick **Direct Messages** and **Group Direct Messages** under **Channel Types**, and has **Index Direct Messages** and **Index Group Direct Messages** instead of **Index Bot Messages**. Click **Save configuration**, then **Start syncing now**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Instead of signing in, you can choose the API token option and paste a **User OAuth Token** (starts with `xoxp-`) from your app's **OAuth & Permissions** page. Use a user token, not a bot token (`xoxb-`): the personal connector needs a user token to read your private channels and direct messages. With token rotation on, a pasted token stops working after about 12 hours, but a Slack sign-in is renewed on its own.
</Note>

<Accordion title="Details for administrators">
  - **Token rotation.** Slack tokens from apps with token rotation expire after about 12 hours. With Slack sign-in (personal connector), PipesHub renews the token on its own. A pasted token (Slack Workspace, or the personal connector's API token option) can't be renewed, so use an app with rotation off. If the logs say **Slack no longer accepts PipesHub's saved sign-in**, the app was reinstalled or removed or the sign-in was revoked: sign in again from the connector's settings.
  - **Scopes the Workspace connector checks.** On save, PipesHub requires the seven required bot scopes above. It notes in its logs when `channels:join` or `usergroups:read` is missing, but saves anyway.
  - **Read-only.** The connector never posts to Slack. The only change it makes is joining public channels when it has `channels:join`. To let agents post to Slack, use the [Slack toolset](/toolsets/slack/slack).
</Accordion>
