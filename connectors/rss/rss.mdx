The RSS connector brings articles from the news sites and blogs you follow into PipesHub, so you can search them and ask questions about them. You give it the addresses of RSS or Atom feeds (the lists of recent posts most sites publish), and PipesHub saves each article. No sign-in or credentials are needed.

<Info>
You can set up RSS as a **team** or a **personal** connector. With a team connector, **everyone in your organization** can search the synced articles. With a personal connector, only you can.
</Info>

## Before you start

- A PipesHub account. To set up a team connector you must be an admin.
- The addresses of the feeds you want. They must be public feeds that open without a login.

## Step 1: Find your feed URLs

<Steps>
  <Step title="Look for the feed link on the site">
    Most news sites and blogs show an RSS icon or an "RSS" or "Feed" link, often in the page footer. Common addresses end in `/feed`, `/rss`, `/rss.xml` or `/atom.xml`, for example `https://blog.example.com/rss`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the address">
    Open the address in your browser. A feed shows a page of text or code listing the latest posts, not the normal website. Copy the full address, including `https://`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 2: Connect RSS in PipesHub

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find the RSS connector">
    For a team connector, open **Workspace** → **Connectors**. For a personal connector, click **Your connectors** on the same page. Search for **RSS** and click **Set up**.

    <img src="/images/connectors/rss/pipeshub-find-connector.png" alt="Connectors page with RSS found by search and its Set up button" style={{ display: 'block', margin: '0 auto' }} width="95%" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the connector">
    On the **Authenticate** tab, enter an **Instance name**, the name the connector will have in PipesHub. The tab shows **No authentication required for this connector**, so there is nothing else to fill. Click **Next**.

    <img src="/images/connectors/rss/pipeshub-authenticate.png" alt="Authenticate tab with the Instance name field and the message No authentication required for this connector" style={{ display: 'block', margin: '0 auto' }} width="60%" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your feeds">
    On the **Configure records** tab, paste your addresses into **Feed URLs**. Separate several feeds with commas or put each on its own line. Then pick a **Sync strategy** and check the other settings (see [Choose what to sync](#choose-what-to-sync)).

    <img src="/images/connectors/rss/pipeshub-configure-records.png" alt="Configure records tab with Sync strategy, Feed URLs, Max Articles Per Feed, Fetch Full Content and Enable Manual Indexing" style={{ display: 'block', margin: '0 auto' }} width="60%" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and start syncing">
    Click **Save configuration**. PipesHub opens each address and checks that it is a feed. If one is not, the error names that address and the reason; fix or remove it and save again.

    When the save succeeds, PipesHub says **Instance is configured and ready to sync!** Click **Start syncing now** to sync right away, or **I'll do it later** to start it later from the connector's settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Choose what to sync

RSS has no sync filters. These are the settings on the **Configure records** tab:

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync strategy** | **Manual** (the default) syncs only when you start a sync. **Scheduled** syncs on its own at a set interval (every hour by default). Pick **Scheduled** to keep news and blog feeds up to date without doing anything. |
| **Feed URLs** | Required. The feeds to sync. Addresses that don't start with `http://` or `https://` are ignored, and repeats are removed. |
| **Max Articles Per Feed** | How many of the newest articles PipesHub reads from each feed on every sync: 1 to 500, 50 by default. |
| **Fetch Full Content** | On by default. PipesHub opens each article's web page and saves the full text. Turn it off to save only the short summary the feed itself contains. That is faster, but less is searchable. |
| **Enable Manual Indexing** | Off by default: every new or changed article is indexed (made searchable). When on, PipesHub syncs new articles but doesn't index them until you choose them in **All records**. An article you already indexed stays indexed, and is indexed again on its own when its text changes. A changed article you never indexed stays unindexed until you choose it. |

## What gets synced and how it stays up to date

- **What you get:** One item per article, with its title, link and text. An article without a link is still saved, using the text in the feed.
- **When it refreshes:** On every sync, PipesHub reads each feed again and saves only new or changed articles. With **Manual**, that happens when you start a sync; with **Scheduled**, at your interval. Articles are not updated in real time.
- **Edited articles:** When an article's text changes, PipesHub saves the new text. Whether it is indexed again depends on **Enable Manual Indexing** (see the table above). A change to only the title or link is saved without indexing the article again.
- **Older articles:** Feeds list only recent posts. An article that drops off the feed stays searchable in PipesHub.
- **Removed feeds:** When you remove an address from **Feed URLs**, the next sync deletes that feed's articles from PipesHub.
- **Not synced:** Files attached to posts, such as podcast audio or PDFs, and feeds or article pages larger than 10 MB.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Saving fails with an error about a feed URL">
The address loads, but it is not an RSS or Atom feed (for example, it is the normal web page or a sitemap), or PipesHub can't reach it. Open the address in your browser and check that it shows a feed. Fix or remove that address and save again.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Some of my feed URLs are ignored">
Only addresses that start with `http://` or `https://` are used. Empty lines and repeated addresses are dropped. Check the spelling and add the `https://` part.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Articles look empty or show the wrong text">
Some sites block automated visitors, need a login, or only show their text after scripts run in a browser. Turn off **Fetch Full Content** to use the feed's own summary instead, or use a feed that includes the full text.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A feed is down or keeps failing">
PipesHub keeps the articles it already has, syncs the other feeds as usual, and sends you a notification naming the failing feed and the reason. It tries the feed again on the next sync. If no feed can be read at all, the sync fails.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="New articles are not appearing">
With the **Manual** sync strategy, PipesHub only syncs when you start a sync. Start one, or switch to **Scheduled**. If **Enable Manual Indexing** is on, new articles are synced but are not searchable until you choose them in **All records**.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="An article was removed from the feed but is still in PipesHub">
That is expected: feeds show only recent posts, so an article that drops off was usually not deleted by the site. To remove all articles of a feed, remove its address from **Feed URLs**; the next sync deletes them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A feed from our internal network is refused">
For safety, PipesHub refuses feeds on private or local network addresses. See **Details for administrators** below.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Details for administrators">
- **Internal feeds:** Feeds on private, loopback or cloud-metadata addresses are refused. To allow a feed from an internal host, list that host name in the `WEB_CONNECTOR_ALLOWED_HOSTS` environment variable (comma-separated) of the PipesHub deployment. Link-local and cloud-metadata addresses stay blocked.
- **Paged feeds:** If a feed splits its posts over several pages, PipesHub reads up to 10 pages per sync, stopping once it reaches **Max Articles Per Feed**.
- **Article identity:** Each article is identified by its ID within its feed, so two feeds that reuse the same IDs keep separate articles.
- **Feeds that need a login:** Feeds behind a login, cookies or other sign-in are not supported.
</Accordion>
