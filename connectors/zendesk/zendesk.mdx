## Overview

The Zendesk connector makes tickets, their public comments and attachments, and published Help Center articles and their attachments searchable in PipesHub. Help Center categories and sections become folders. Zendesk agents, admins, groups, and organizations are synced to work out who can see each ticket. Internal ticket notes and Help Center articles restricted to a user segment are not synced.

<Note>
  The connector signs in with OAuth only. Zendesk is removing Support API tokens (used by the Ticketing and Help Center APIs): accounts can't create new ones from October 27, 2026, and all remaining Support API tokens stop working on April 30, 2027. Messaging and Chat API tokens are not affected. See [Zendesk's announcement](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/10851263566234-Announcing-the-removal-of-API-tokens-as-an-authentication-method-for-API-requests).
</Note>

## Set up Zendesk

You need a **Zendesk admin account** and access to **Workspace Settings** in PipesHub. Use the same email address in both apps so your access is matched correctly.

### 1. Copy the redirect URL

In PipesHub, go to **Workspace Settings** → **Connectors** → **Zendesk** → **Set up**. On the **Authenticate** tab, enter an **Instance name** (required) and copy the **Redirect/Callback URL**.

### 2. Create an OAuth client in Zendesk

In Zendesk **Admin Center**, go to **Apps and integrations** → **APIs** → **OAuth clients** → **Add OAuth client**. Give it a name, then set:

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Client kind** | **Confidential** |
| **Redirect URLs** | Paste the exact URL copied from PipesHub |
| **Scopes** | Allow `read` if you restrict client scopes |

Save the client. Copy its **Identifier** and **Secret**; Zendesk displays the full secret only once. See [Zendesk's OAuth client instructions](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408845965210-Using-OAuth-authentication-with-your-application) if you need help with this step.

### 3. Enter the client details in PipesHub

Return to the **Authenticate** tab. If you have already saved a Zendesk OAuth app in PipesHub, you can pick it from the OAuth app list. Otherwise choose **Create new OAuth app**, give it an **OAuth app name**, and enter:

| Field | Value for `acme.zendesk.com` |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| **Subdomain** | `acme` |
| **Authorize URL** | `https://acme.zendesk.com/oauth/authorizations/new` |
| **Token URL** | `https://acme.zendesk.com/oauth/tokens` |
| **Client ID** | The Zendesk client's **Identifier** |
| **Client Secret** | The Zendesk client's **Secret** |

Replace `acme` with your Zendesk subdomain, then click **Next**. PipesHub always signs in at `https://<subdomain>.zendesk.com`, so enter your `zendesk.com` subdomain even if your help center uses a custom domain.

### 4. Authorize and start syncing

On the **Authorize** tab, click **Authenticate Zendesk to proceed**, sign in with a **Zendesk admin account**, and approve access. Then click **Continue to configuration →**.

On the **Configure records** tab, choose **Scheduled** (every 60 minutes by default) or **Manual** sync, adjust filters if needed, and click **Save configuration**. In the **Instance is configured and ready to sync!** dialog, select **Start syncing now** to begin.

<Note>
  Leave **Groups** empty to sync tickets from all groups. When you select groups, tickets with no group are included only if you also select **Unassigned**. The **Modified Date** and **Created Date** filters limit tickets and articles. The **Index Tickets**, **Index Attachments** (ticket and article attachments), and **Index Knowledge Base** switches control what appears in search. With **Enable Manual Indexing** on, records sync but aren't indexed until you choose them.
</Note>

## Permissions

- **Tickets** are visible in PipesHub to Zendesk admins and agents who can see them in Zendesk, for example agents with access to all tickets, members of the ticket's group, the assignee, and agents named as CCs or followers. Once a ticket has a public comment, its requester and CCs can also see it, as can members of the ticket's organization when that organization shares tickets.
- **Help Center articles**, categories, and sections are visible to everyone in your PipesHub organization, because only published articles with no user-segment restriction are synced. This also applies when your whole Help Center is restricted to signed-in users: PipesHub doesn't read that setting, so everyone in your PipesHub organization can still find those articles.
- Tickets deleted in Zendesk, and articles that are deleted, archived, unpublished, or later restricted to a user segment, are removed from PipesHub.

## Troubleshooting

- **Authorization fails:** Check that the redirect URL matches exactly, the Client ID is the Zendesk **Identifier**, and the Secret is complete.
- **Sync requires an admin:** Re-authorize with a Zendesk admin account. An agent account cannot run the required exports.
- **Content is missing:** Review the group and date filters. Only public ticket comments and published Help Center articles without user-segment restrictions are synced; also check the indexing switches.
