## Current status

Google Meet is registered as a **beta connector definition**. It appears in the connector catalogue and has a complete OAuth configuration, but the sync itself is not implemented.

<Warning>
**This connector does not index anything yet.** Its definition sets
`with_sync_support(False)` (`core/registry/connector.py:193`), and `MeetConnector.connect()` is a placeholder that
returns without contacting the API. Setting it up will not make Google Meet content
searchable in PipesHub.
</Warning>

The definition is present so the connector appears in the catalogue and its OAuth
application can be registered ahead of the sync implementation landing. It is also
gated behind the beta connector registry, so it only appears when beta connectors
are enabled for your deployment.

## What works today

Nothing in this connector reaches Google Meet yet. If you need this data now, the
practical options are:

- **Google Drive connector** — Forms and Meet recordings that are stored as Drive
  files are indexed by the [Google Drive connector](/connectors/google-workspace/drive/drive)
  like any other file.
- **Google Calendar** — meeting details attached to calendar events are covered by
  the [Google Calendar page](/connectors/google-workspace/calendar/calendar).

## For when it ships

The OAuth application it will use is already defined, so this part is stable and
worth setting up in advance if you are preparing an environment.

Google Meet requires **two** Google APIs enabled, not one:

- **Google Meet API**
- **Google Calendar API** — a Meet space is reached through the calendar event that hosts it

Enabling only the obvious one is the common mistake, and it fails quietly.

The redirect URI ends with the capitalised connector name and must be registered
exactly as PipesHub displays it.
