## Overview

PipesHub has two Notion connectors. Both sync **pages**, **databases**, **comments**, and **file attachments** from the pages you share with the connection. They use the same Notion developer setup, but they do **not** share the same audience in search.

| Connector | Where you set it up | Who can search the synced records |
|-----------|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Notion** (team) | **Workspace settings → Connectors → Notion** | Each person who [links their own Notion personal access token](#your-notion-personal-access-token): only the synced pages that token can open in Notion. Without a token, none |
| **Notion Personal** | **Workspace settings → Your connectors → Notion Personal** | **Only you** (the person who created the connector) |

<Warning>
  **Team connector access:** Notion's API does not say who may open a page (page sharing, teamspace membership and guests are not exposed). So the team connector does **not** make its pages searchable by the whole workspace. A person sees a synced page in PipesHub only once their own linked Notion personal access token can open it. Without one, a person sees no Notion pages. Use **Notion Personal** to search the pages you share with it.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The team connector identifies people by **email**: your personal access token must belong to the Notion account with the **same email** you use to sign in to PipesHub. Notion Personal does not need a matching email. The PipesHub user who created the connector is its only audience.
</Note>

### What gets synced

| Entity | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Pages** | Pages you grant during OAuth (or share with an internal connection), including their blocks |
| **Databases** | Database properties and rows in scope (Notion data sources) |
| **Files** | Attachments on pages and comment attachments (when **Index Files** is on) |
| **Comments** | Comments on shared pages, when the connection can read comments |
| **Users** | Team connector only - workspace members, matched to PipesHub users by verified email. Notion Personal does not sync a user directory |

The connector only sees content Notion granted it. **Configure records** in PipesHub can narrow indexing further; it cannot reach pages you never shared.

### Authentication

| Method | Team (**Notion**) | **Notion Personal** |
|--------|-------------------|---------------------|
| **OAuth** (New connection → OAuth) | Yes - redirect path ends with `/Notion` | Yes - redirect path ends with `/Notion%20Personal` |
| **Access token** (`ntn_...`) | Yes - same **New connection** dialog, **Access token** method | No |
| **Personal access tokens** (Developer tools tab) | Not as the connection's credential. Each user links their own to decide what they can search: see [Your Notion personal access token](#your-notion-personal-access-token) | Not supported |

In PipesHub the OAuth flow has three tabs: **Authenticate** (Redirect URL + client credentials), **Authorize** (sign in to Notion and pick pages), then **Configure records** (sync strategy and indexing).

## Configuration Guide

In Notion, open **Settings → Connections → Go to developer portal**, create a connection, copy the **Redirect URL** from PipesHub into that connection (OAuth only), then finish setup in PipesHub.

   - **Personal** - under **Personal**, select **Your connectors**. Find **Notion Personal** and click **+ Set up**.

### Team alternative: internal connection

The **team** connector can use an **Access token** (Internal Integration Secret) instead of OAuth. Notion Personal does not support this. Tokens from the **Personal access tokens** tab in Developer tools are **not** accepted - the workspace is identified from the connection's bot user.

1. Follow [Step 3](#step-3-open-notions-developer-portal) and click **+ New connection**.
2. Choose **Access token** (workspace-scoped static API token, one workspace). Fill **Connection name** and create the connection. Redirect URIs are not used.
3. On the connection page, copy the **Access token** (`ntn_...`) and set the same **Capabilities** as Step 5 (read content, read comments, user email).

## Access and permissions

| | **Notion** (team) | **Notion Personal** |
|--|-------------------|---------------------|
| **Who can search** | Each person with a linked personal access token, for the synced pages that token can open | Only the connector creator |
| **Follows Notion page access** | Yes, through each person's own personal access token. Without one, a person sees no pages | No per-page access either - but the only audience is you |
| **User directory** | Synced to match people by email | Not synced |
| **How to widen or narrow content** | Share more or fewer pages with the connection (OAuth picker or internal **Content access**) | Same - only the pages you share during authorization |

### Your Notion personal access token

A Notion [personal access token](https://developers.notion.com/guides/connections-and-tokens/personal-access-tokens) acts as the person who created it, with that person's own page permissions. The team connector uses it to find out what you may open in Notion:

- At each sync, PipesHub checks that the token belongs to you (the same verified email as your PipesHub sign-in) in the connector's Notion workspace, then searches Notion with it.
- Each page or database the search lists is then opened with your token, and you can search only the synced ones that open. A listing alone is not enough: Notion's search index [takes time to reflect changes and is not a complete inventory](https://developers.notion.com/reference/search-optimizations-and-limitations). Files follow their page.
- New pages are confirmed over the following syncs, up to 2,000 per person per sync. Pages you can already search are re-checked oldest first, from about 20 hours after their last check, spread over the syncs so that each is checked again before its confirmation runs out, and all of them on a **Full sync**.
- A confirmation lasts 24 hours. A page not re-checked within 24 hours of its last confirmation is hidden from you until a later sync confirms it again.
- A database's table is shown only to people who may open every row of it. Each row stays searchable on its own.
- A token is removed and you get a notification when Notion refuses it (revoked or expired), when it has no **Notion API** access, when it belongs to someone else or another workspace, or when its creator is no longer a member of the connector's Notion workspace with a verified email. Link a new one to see your Notion pages again.
- If PipesHub cannot tell this sync whether the token's creator is still a member (the member list could not be read in full, or Notion showed no email for them), the token is kept and gives you no pages until a later sync can tell.
- If Notion cannot be read with your token during a sync, the pages it gave you are hidden until a later sync succeeds.
- Admins can see who linked a token and its state on the connector's **Overview** tab, never the token itself.

Guests and restricted members cannot create a [personal access token](https://developers.notion.com/guides/connections-and-tokens/personal-access-tokens), so they do not see this connector's pages in PipesHub. Workspace admins can limit who may create one: by default workspace owners only on Free and Business, all members on Plus, and owners and selected groups on Enterprise. A token expires, after 1 year by default.

To link your token:

1. In Notion, open **Settings → Connections → Go to developer portal**, then the **Personal access tokens** tab, and select **New token**.
2. Name the token, give it **Notion API** access, and choose an expiration (up to 1 year). If Notion asks for a workspace, pick the one this connector syncs.
3. Select **Create token** and copy the value. Notion shows it only once.
4. In PipesHub, open **Workspace settings → Your connectors**. Paste the token in the **Your Notion access** panel for this connector and click **Link**. Admins also find this panel on the connector's **Overview** tab.

The token takes effect at the connector's next sync. Use **Replace** before it expires, or **Remove** to unlink it.

#### What the panel shows

The token field hides what you type, and PipesHub never shows a linked token again, to you or to an admin. Under the field, the panel shows your token's state:

| State | What it means |
|-------|---------------|
| **Not linked** | You have no token on this connector, so you see none of its pages. |
| **Linked on** *date* | Your token is linked. The pages it can open appear over the following syncs. |
| **Stopped working:** *reason* | A sync removed your token for the reason shown, for example that Notion no longer accepts it. You also get a notification. Link a new token to see your Notion pages again. |

**Remove** asks you to confirm first. Removing deletes PipesHub's copy of your token, and by the connector's next sync you no longer see the pages it opened. The token itself keeps working in Notion until you revoke it: token creators revoke their own tokens in Notion's developer portal ([Notion: personal access tokens](https://developers.notion.com/guides/connections-and-tokens/personal-access-tokens)).

On the connector's **Overview** tab, admins also see **Who linked a token**: each person by name, with the same state. Admins never see a token and cannot remove someone else's. PipesHub drops a person's token itself at the next sync after they leave the organization or their account is deactivated.

## Current limitations

Each sync uses Notion's Search API and reads the pages and databases whose **`last_edited_time`** is newer than the last successful run, plus a ten-minute overlap. At the end of every sync, PipesHub also compares what it holds with everything the connection can see now:

- Pages and databases shared with the connection later are synced, even if nobody edited them.
- Pages and databases that are deleted, moved to the trash, unshared, or moved under a page the connection cannot see are removed from PipesHub.

A record is removed only when Notion confirms the page is gone or in the trash. If Notion returns an error or the listing does not finish, nothing is removed that run and the next sync tries again.

| Situation | What a sync does | What to do |
|-----------|------------------|------------|
| **Move** a page or database to a new parent that the connection can still see (no content edit) | Usually nothing. A move alone often does not update `last_edited_time`, and Notion has no API for move events. | Edit the page or database, or run **Full sync**. |
| **Add or delete a comment** | Notion does not update a page's `last_edited_time` for comments. Each sync rechecks the comments of a slice of pages, so a change is picked up within about 24 syncs (about a day at the default 1-hour interval; longer on very large workspaces). | Edit the page, or run **Full sync**, if you need it sooner. |

Other limits that come from the same Notion API:

- **Team access needs a personal access token per person.** Notion does not return who a page is shared with, so each person links their own token. See [Your Notion personal access token](#your-notion-personal-access-token).
- **Not realtime.** There is no webhook-driven sync. Changes appear on the next scheduled run, a manual **Sync**, or a **Full sync**.
- **Size limits.** Images added by URL are fetched up to **20 MB**, and files added by URL up to **100 MB**. Larger ones are not indexed.
- **Only pages the connection can see.** Search never returns content you did not share with the integration.
- **Rate limit.** Notion allows an average of about **3 requests per second** per connection on most plans (some paid plans allow more). Large pages need many block fetches, so the first sync of a big workspace can take a long time.
- **Images are not separate files.** Inline images stay on the page (and may be inlined for indexing). **Index Files** covers file, video, PDF, and audio attachments - not image blocks.
- **Lost access can lag, up to a day.** A page someone can no longer open in Notion can stay searchable for them until its re-check, and at most 24 hours after it was last confirmed plus the time to the next sync. Notion sends no event when sharing changes, so PipesHub can't learn of it sooner.
- **Publishing a page gives no access.** A page published to the web is searchable in PipesHub only by people whose own token can open it.
- **Content from other pages is left out.** On the team connector, a page's indexed content leaves out what comes from pages that may have other readers: titles of linked, child and mentioned pages and databases, the titles Notion shows for pasted links (the URL is kept), relation titles (the target's id is kept), rollups, the formulas of a page that relates to other pages, and synced copies of another page's blocks.
- **After upgrading, pages come back once re-indexed.** Pages synced by an earlier version are indexed again so that their content leaves out the items above. Each stays hidden until its new index finishes, so pages reappear over the following syncs.
- **Very large workspaces may briefly lose pages.** PipesHub reads Notion with everyone's tokens through one shared limit of **3 requests per second**, Notion's documented average for most plans ([request limits](https://developers.notion.com/reference/request-limits)). Re-checks are planned within half of that, about **5,400 an hour**, to leave room for the searches and the content sync. If more pages than that need re-checking in time, some can drop out of search until they are re-checked, at the latest on the next **Full sync**.
- **Users without a verified email.** The team connector matches a Notion member to a PipesHub user only if Notion reports their email as verified ([`email_verified`](https://developers.notion.com/reference/user)). A token whose creator is not matched grants nothing until they are. If Notion returns no email for anyone, the sync stops and asks you to enable the email capability.
- **Personal access tokens** from Developer tools are not a supported auth method for the connection itself. They are only for each person's own access.

<Accordion title="Troubleshooting" icon="circle-exclamation">

### Common issues

**Redirect URI mismatch**

- The value in Notion must **exactly** match the Redirect URL shown in PipesHub (scheme, host, path, no stray spaces).
- Team and Personal use different paths (`/Notion` vs `/Notion%20Personal`). Register the URI for the connector you are setting up.

**Invalid client / secret**

- Regenerate the secret in Notion if needed and paste the new value into **Authenticate**.

**Sync failed with a token or capability message**

| Message | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| *Notion rejected the API token: it is invalid or was revoked.* | Copy a current **Access token** from the connection and paste it on **Authenticate**. |
| *The Notion integration is missing the … capability.* | Enable the named capability (**Read content**, **Read comments**, or **Read user information including email addresses**) on the connection, then sync again. |
| *Notion token is missing the … capability* or *Notion OAuth token is no longer active* | Enable the capability on the connection, then use **Re-authenticate with provider** on **Authorize**. |
| *Notion returned no email address for any workspace member.* | Enable **Read user information including email addresses** on the connection, then sync again. |

PipesHub also sends a notification when a sync fails, and a warning when some pages could not be read. Those pages are retried on the next sync.

**Internal secret rejected**

- Use an **internal connection** installation token (`ntn_...`), not a Notion personal access token.

**Missing pages after sync**

- Re-run **Authorize** and add the pages, or share them with an internal connection (**Content access** or **Add connection**). The connector only sees content you explicitly share.
- Pages you share later are picked up at the end of the next sync. If they are still missing after a sync finishes, check the instance for a sync warning. See [Current limitations](#current-limitations).

**I see no Notion pages (team)**

- Link your own personal access token in the **Your Notion access** panel under **Workspace settings → Your connectors**, then wait for the next sync. See [Your Notion personal access token](#your-notion-personal-access-token).
- If you got a notification that your Notion access token no longer works, it was revoked, expired, lacks **Notion API** access, or belongs to someone else or to another Notion workspace. Link a new one.
- A page must be both synced by the connection and open to you in Notion. A newly shared page appears once a sync opens it with your token (up to 2,000 new pages per person per sync).

**Email / visibility**

- Confirm **Capabilities** include **user information with email**, and that people sign in to PipesHub with the same address as in Notion.
- On Notion Personal, only the PipesHub user who created the instance can search its records. Other users will not see them.

**Reindex does nothing / manual indexing stuck**

- Reindex is supported for pages, databases, and file attachments. After you change **Index Pages**, **Index Databases**, or **Index Files**, reindex the affected records so the new toggles apply.

**Sync is slow or hits Notion limits**

- Notion allows an average of about **3 requests per second** per connection on most plans. Large pages (many blocks) need many API calls. The first sync of a big workspace can take a long time; later incremental runs only fetch changes.

</Accordion>

## Data sync behavior

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Synchronization process" icon="sync">

### Initial sync

- Imports pages and databases in the granted scope, plus comments and file attachments when those capabilities and indexing toggles are on.
- Duration depends on workspace size and Notion's rate limit.

### Incremental sync

- Runs on your **Sync interval** when using a scheduled strategy. It fetches pages and databases with a newer **`last_edited_time`** than the last successful run.
- At the end of each sync, newly shared pages are added and deleted, trashed, or unshared pages are removed.
- Comment changes and moves without an edit can lag. See [Current limitations](#current-limitations).
- Not realtime. Use manual **Sync** or **Full sync** when you need an immediate refresh.

### Reindex

- Re-fetches selected pages, databases (data sources), and file attachments and republishes them for indexing.

## FAQ

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Queries not returning results. What could be wrong?">

1. **Wrong connector** - Team records are visible only to people who linked their own Notion personal access token. Personal records are visible only to the creator.
2. **Email mismatch** - On the team connector, your token must belong to the Notion account with the same email as in PipesHub.
3. **Scope** - Confirm the right pages were selected during **Authorize** (or shared with the internal connection) and that indexing toggles match what you expect.
4. **Still indexing** - Open the instance **Overview** until queues complete.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Should I use Notion or Notion Personal?">

Use **Notion Personal** to search your own pages: only you can search what it syncs.

Use the **team** connector when many people should search one synced workspace. Each person links their own Notion personal access token and sees only the pages they can open in Notion.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does the team connector honor Notion page permissions?">

Yes, through each person's own personal access token. Notion's API does not say who a page is shared with, so PipesHub asks Notion what each person's token can open and grants exactly those pages. People without a token, including guests, see no pages from the team connector. A page someone loses access to can stay searchable for them for up to a day; see [Current limitations](#current-limitations).

</Accordion>
