PipesHub ranks **#1** on the EnterpriseRAG-Bench leaderboard with an overall score of **93.24**.

## What the benchmark is

EnterpriseRAG-Bench is a retrieval benchmark for company internal knowledge, released by Onyx. It has **500 questions** over a corpus of about **500,000 documents** from nine kinds of company source. Teams run the 500 questions through their own system and submit the answers. The leaderboard is hosted on Hugging Face.

- [Leaderboard on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/spaces/onyx-dot-app/EnterpriseRAG-Bench-Leaderboard)
- [Benchmark and harness on GitHub](https://github.com/onyx-dot-app/EnterpriseRAG-Bench)
- [Paper on arXiv (2605.05253)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05253)

## What this does and does not show

- **Submitted results.** Each system on the leaderboard was run by its own team on the 500-question test set and submitted for verification. The leaderboard is not a single lab running every system.
- **A fixed question set.** Scores are for these 500 questions and this corpus, with the benchmark's grading. They do not predict how any system does on your documents.
- **The board moves.** New systems are added over time and ranks can change. Check the [live leaderboard](https://huggingface.co/spaces/onyx-dot-app/EnterpriseRAG-Bench-Leaderboard) for the current standings.
