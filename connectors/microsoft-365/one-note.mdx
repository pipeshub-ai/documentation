icon: "/logo/one-note.png"
---

<Note>
  PipesHub has no OneNote connector or toolset, so OneNote notebooks are not synced or indexed.
</Note>

To search Microsoft 365 content, use the [OneDrive](/connectors/microsoft-365/one-drive) or [SharePoint](/connectors/microsoft-365/sharepoint) connector. See the [connectors overview](/connectors/overview) for everything that is available.
