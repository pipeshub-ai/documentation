Accuracy on your own documents is the first thing to check in any enterprise AI search or enterprise RAG platform. This page lists the results PipesHub has published, who ran each test, what is and is not public about it, and how to repeat a test on your own data.

### Read these results carefully

- **EnterpriseRAG-Bench** is the only result here that a third party ran and published. Its maintainer also builds an enterprise search product. The leaderboard records the score at the date linked above; check the live board for the current standing.
- **FRAMES, the connector test and the code-graph test** were run by the PipesHub team. Each write-up describes the setup. The code-graph harness is [public](https://github.com/pipeshub-ai/codegraph-bench), so you can rerun it. The connector test uses questions about PipesHub's own internal work, so it cannot be reproduced from public data.
- No benchmark measures your documents, your permissions or your users' questions. Treat any published number, including these, as a starting point and run your own test before you decide.

## Measure accuracy on your own documents

A test on a few hundred real questions tells you more than any public leaderboard. You need a PipesHub instance with your sources connected and indexed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Collect questions with known answers">
    Ask 5–10 people from different teams for 20–30 real questions each, with the document that answers each one. Include questions that need several documents, questions with dates or numbers, and questions the asker should **not** be able to answer because of permissions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask each question as the person who wrote it">
    Use each person's own login or [personal access token](/developer/personal-access-tokens), so results reflect their permissions. Use the chat API for full answers, or the [Retrieval API](/developer/retrieval-api) (`POST /api/v1/retrieve`) to score retrieval alone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Score three things per question">
    - **Retrieval:** did the expected document appear in the cited sources?
    - **Answer:** is the answer correct and complete? Have the question's author grade it.
    - **Permissions:** for the questions the asker should not see, confirm nothing from the restricted document appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-run after changes">
    Keep the question set. Re-run it after you change models, connectors or settings, so you can see whether quality moved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Permissions are checked when a question runs, against the permissions PipesHub has synced from each source. See [Permissions and freshness by connector](/connectors/permissions-matrix) for how quickly changes made in a source reach search, and test a revoked-access case as part of your question set.
</Note>
