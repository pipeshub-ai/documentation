<Note>
  PipesHub has no Microsoft Teams connector, so Teams chats and channels are not synced or indexed.
</Note>

Agents can work with Microsoft Teams through the **Microsoft Teams toolset**, which reads and posts to channels, chats, and meetings on behalf of the signed-in user. See the [Microsoft Teams toolset](/toolsets/microsoft-365/teams) for setup and the available actions.

To learn how connectors and toolsets differ, see the [connectors overview](/connectors/overview).
