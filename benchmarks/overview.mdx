## Terms used on these pages

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="RAG and agent loop">
    **RAG** (retrieval-augmented generation) searches your documents once, hands the best passages to a model and asks it to answer. An **agent loop** lets the model search, read, search again and then answer, so it can follow a question across several documents.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Multi-hop question">
    A question that needs facts from several documents, so one search is not enough. For example, "Who manages the person who wrote the pricing page?"
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Grounded">
    An answer that is correct and also supported by text the system actually read, not recalled from the model's memory.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Index">
    The searchable copy of your apps' content that PipesHub's connectors build. See [Connectors, MCP servers and Actions](/connectors-mcp-actions).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## How to read these results

- **Who ran it.** Some results are on an independent leaderboard that PipesHub submitted to. Others PipesHub ran itself. Each page says which.
- **Dates matter.** Leaderboards and tools change. Each page states when its figures were read.
- **Sources are linked.** Every number comes from a linked source: the leaderboard, a PipesHub blog post or the benchmark's repository.
- **A benchmark is not your data.** Results depend on the questions, the models and the corpus. Test PipesHub on your own content before you rely on a number.
